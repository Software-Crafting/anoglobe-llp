Overlays

Layers that sit above the page. All of them are our own: no native title tooltips, no browser dialogs.

Command palette

Press Ctrl K (Cmd K on Mac) anywhere in the admin to jump to a page, a client or an action.

Open the palette

The palette is a modal combobox: type to filter, arrows to move, Enter to open.
