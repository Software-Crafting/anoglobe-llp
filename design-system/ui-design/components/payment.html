Components

Payment

Interface only. The real gateway fields replace the card element later; card data is never stored by Anoglobe.

Card payment

The card element stands in for the gateway's hosted fields. It detects the brand, checks the number, expiry and code, and walks through processing, success and decline.

Pay with card

Test mode. Use 4242 4242 4242 4242 to succeed or 4000 0000 0000 0002 to see a decline, any future date and any CVC.

Card details

Card details go straight to Stripe and are never stored by Anoglobe.
