Services

Anoglobe LLP provides the bookkeeping services described in the plan you choose. The scope of each plan is listed on the pricing page at the time you sign up.

Billing

Monthly plans are billed in US dollars on the same day each month, starting on the day you sign up. One-time projects are billed once when you confirm the project.

  • Receipts are sent by email after every charge.
  • Price changes are announced at least 30 days in advance.

Cancelling

You can cancel a monthly plan any time before the next billing date. Work already completed for a billed month is not refunded.

Your responsibilities

You agree to give us accurate information, timely access to your accounts and answers to our monthly questions.

Limits

Our reports are based on the information you provide. We are not responsible for errors caused by missing or inaccurate information.

Governing law

These terms are governed by the laws named in your service agreement.
