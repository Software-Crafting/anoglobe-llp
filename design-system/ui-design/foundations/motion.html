Motion

Quick, quiet and optional.

Durations and easing

Three durations and one ease out curve. Motion confirms a change; it never delays a task.

Play

micro --duration-micro 120msHover, focus, color changes
standard --duration-standard 200msPopovers, toasts, chevrons
panel --duration-panel 320msSheets, sidebar, drawers

Reduced motion

When the system asks for reduced motion, durations drop to near zero and looping animations stop. Nothing depends on motion to make sense.

  • Tokens set every duration to 0.01ms under prefers-reduced-motion.
  • base.css also stops all animations and transitions as a safety net.
  • Spinners, ringing and pulsing icons keep a static state that still reads correctly.
