Imagery

Real, calm photography of small business work, and our own spot illustrations.

Photo treatment

Calm, real scenes of small business work: owners at desks, documents, quiet offices. Slightly desaturated, rounded xl corners, and a soft mist overlay only where text sits on top.

  • Ratios: 16:9 for wide hero bands, 4:3 for split sections, 1:1 for avatars and cards.
  • Avoid clichés: handshakes, piles of cash, stock charts over laptops, staged high fives.
  • Show real work and real places. No AI generated images presented as photos.
  • Every image is downloaded into assets/img, sized for its slot (1600px wide for heroes, 800px for cards), with width, height and meaningful alt text.
  • Record every photo in assets/img/CREDITS.md with its source and photographer.
