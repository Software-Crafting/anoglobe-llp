Typography

One family, a nine step scale, and tabular figures for money.

Typeface

Inter is the only family, self hosted as a variable font with optical sizing. Codes and IDs use the system monospace stack.

Writing rules

Plain, calm US business English.

  • Short sentences. Labels in sentence case. No hype words.
  • No em dashes or en dashes. Use commas, periods and colons.
  • No emojis anywhere in the product or emails.
  • Errors say what to do: "Enter a 9 digit EIN, like 12-3456789", never "Invalid".
  • Dates as Oct 21, 2026. Times as 10:42 AM ET.
